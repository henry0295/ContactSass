# Product Overview

ContactSass is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for mass communication campaigns across Email, SMS, and Voice Broadcasting channels. Designed for horizontal scalability to process campaigns with millions of messages.

## Core Capabilities

- Multi-tenant contact and list management
- Multi-channel campaign creation and scheduling (Email, SMS, Voice)
- Distributed worker architecture for large-scale message delivery
- Real-time delivery tracking and analytics
- Usage metering and billing cycle management

## Target Scale

- Campaigns with millions of messages
- Horizontal worker scaling per channel
- Tenant-isolated data and rate limiting
- Provider-level throughput management (SES, SNS, FreeSWITCH)

## Key Business Domains

- Campaign orchestration and batching
- Multi-channel messaging (email/sms/voice)
- Delivery event tracking
- Usage-based billing
- Tenant management and RBAC
